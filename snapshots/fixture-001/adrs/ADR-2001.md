# ADR-2001: TLS termination at edge

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Public traffic enters through a shared edge. Teams previously terminated TLS in app pods, which fragmented certificate lifecycle.

## Decision
All public HTTP traffic must terminate TLS at the edge proxy with certificates managed by the platform.

## Consequences
- Apps speak plain HTTP on the mesh side of the edge.
- Certificate renewal is centralized.
- Low-confidence follow-ups may need human review when scope language is broad.
